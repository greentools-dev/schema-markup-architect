        Schema.org Structured Data FAQ
      


      

        

          
Why use JSON-LD over Microdata?

          

            Google explicitly recommends JSON-LD because it is isolated inside a <script> tag in the head or body, keeping your HTML markup clean without interfering with web components or CSS styling.
          

        

        

          
How do rich results impact CTR?

          

            Pages displaying review stars, stock status, and FAQ accordions take up 2x to 3x more vertical screen real estate on Google SERPs, consistently increasing organic click-through rates by 20% to 35%.
          

        

        

          
Can I nest multiple schemas together?

          

            Yes. Using Schema.org @graph arrays, you can easily combine Product, FAQPage, and BreadcrumbList schemas into a single snippet without conflicts.
          

        

        

          
Is this tool free with zero server logs?

          

            Yes. The generator and previewer execute 100% inside your browser memory. We never store, log, or transmit your product data or API credentials.
